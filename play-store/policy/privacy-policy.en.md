# Paper Craft Print Privacy Policy Draft

Status: review draft before publication. The effective date will be set when the public page is published. The planned address is `https://www.seorilabs.com/apps/paper-craft-print/privacy/`; publication and the HTTP response have not been verified.

## App and contact

Paper Craft Print is an Android paper template editor distributed by Seolee Apps for adult hobbyists. Contact `cs@seorilabs.com` with privacy questions.

## Information kept on your device

The app stores drafts, notes, dimensions, pattern choices, copies of selected photos, generated PDFs, language preferences and rewarded pattern permissions on your device. It has no sign-in, app server, cloud synchronization, Firebase, separate product analytics or payments. Selected photos and drafts are not uploaded to the app operator's server.

You select photos through the system photo picker. The app does not request access to your entire photo library, camera or microphone. Selected photos are used to edit your craft and create its PDF.

## Saving, sharing and printing

At your request, the app saves a PDF to a location you choose with the system folder picker or passes it to system sharing and printing features. The receiving app, service or printer handles the file under its own terms. Files saved or sent outside this app are not deleted by this app.

## Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob banners may appear on Home and My drafts. You can choose a rewarded ad to open an additional pattern for a particular draft. The app initializes Google Mobile Ads after checking that ad requests are permitted. You can use all free templates and basic patterns and create PDFs when advertising is unavailable or not permitted.

Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform may process IP addresses, advertising interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers to provide and measure ads, diagnose problems and prevent abuse. An IP address may be used to estimate a general location. User data collected by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Processing may vary with your region and privacy choices.

Use Ad privacy choices in Settings to review or change the choices available to you. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) for Google's practices and controls. The advertising provider's policies govern its retention and deletion procedures.

## Retention and deletion

Drafts remain on your device until you delete them or clear app data. Copies of photos used only by a deleted draft are cleaned up when no other draft refers to them. Deleting a draft does not delete PDFs already created inside the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes internal app data. Delete files saved to external folders or other services at those locations. Android automatic backup is disabled.

## Audience and updates

The app is intended for adult hobbyists and is not designed for children. If features or data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date on the public page.
